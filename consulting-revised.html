Scientific Advisory & Technical Consulting

Work with engineering teams on the geometry underneath their estimation, control, and simulation code: rotations and rigid-body motions that do not live in a vector space, filters that degrade when they are treated as though they do, and gradients that stop being trustworthy over long horizons.

Engagements

Where teams usually call

Each of these can be taken as a scoped review with a written report, or as ongoing advisory alongside your team.

RB

Geometry of rotation and rigid-body motion

Symptoms: normalization steps scattered through the codebase; state that has to be cleaned up every few timesteps; controllers that behave differently depending on how the attitude happens to be parameterized.

Review of how rotations and rigid motions are represented, propagated, and differentiated in your stack, with concrete recommendations on parameterization, integrator choice, and the interfaces between subsystems that use different conventions.

EST

Estimation and filtering on manifolds

Symptoms: an estimator that is well-behaved in simulation and drifts in flight; covariances that are optimistic under aggressive maneuvers; tuning that has to be redone for every new trajectory.

Assessment of filter formulation and consistency, covering error-state design, the case for and against an invariant formulation, observability under your actual sensor suite, and a consistency test procedure your team can run without me.

ADJ

Adjoint and sensitivity correctness

Symptoms: an optimizer that stalls or takes steps the objective does not reward; gradients that disagree with finite differences and no clear reason why; sensitivity computations that diverge as the horizon lengthens.

Audit of how gradients are formed and whether they are the gradients of the quantity actually being minimized, covering discrete versus continuous adjoint consistency, verification against independent checks, and the discretization choices that keep long-horizon sensitivities bounded.

Areas of expertise

The underlying capability

DT

Digital Twin Architecture

Building rigorous mathematical foundations for hierarchical digital twins: feedback loops that tightly couple physical systems with high-fidelity simulations via data assimilation and decision control.

GN

Geometric Numerical Methods

Structure-preserving numerical discretizations for simulation stability, in particular variational integrators that preserve momentum and symplectic structure over long time horizons.

AI

Physics-Informed ML

System identification and training of data-aware physics-informed models, using adjoint sensitivity analysis that respects the symmetries of the underlying system.

OP

Accelerated Optimization

Symplectic optimization algorithms built from discrete constrained variational integrators, aimed at improved convergence behavior on structured problems.

The Geometric Advantage

Manifold Awareness

Standard methods struggle on the nonlinear manifolds (Lie groups and homogeneous spaces) where robots and drones actually operate. A geometric approach preserves these configurations natively.

Computational Efficiency

The implicit bottleneck can be addressed with geometric exponential integrators and predictor-corrector methods that are computationally competitive with traditional methods.

Long-Term Qualitative Accuracy

By discretizing Hamilton's principle directly, variational integrators eliminate numerical dissipation and keep energy behavior stable over extended time horizons.
